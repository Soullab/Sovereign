FOUNDER ADJUDICATION — `ELEMENTAL-CONSCIOUSNESS-ARCHITECTURE-01 / ECA-00`

**I. DISPOSITION**

Accept `ECA-00 — CONSTITUTIONAL INTEGRATION ONLY` as:

SUBSTANTIVELY PASS · FOUNDATIONAL ARCHITECTURE ACCEPTED · TWO NON-SUBSTANTIVE EDITORIAL REPAIRS REQUIRED BEFORE FINAL FREEZE

The constitutional architecture is accepted.

No runtime authority, empirical standing, benchmark authority, deployment authority, neurological localization claim, or RGR evidentiary expansion is thereby granted.

**II. ACCEPTED CONSTITUTIONAL LAWS**

The following architecture is accepted as foundational:

* Fire, Water, Earth, and Air are differentiated elemental modes of participation in reality.
* Their correspondence with Jungian Intuition, Feeling, Sensation, and Thinking belongs to Elemental Alchemy and shall not be attributed to Jung as an elemental theory.
* Element and hemisphere are orthogonal dimensions: element concerns differentiated mode; hemispheric framing concerns manner of attention and participation.
* No element is neurologically identified with one hemisphere.
* The Fifth occupies a different logical level from the Four and names the larger relational whole within which differentiation occurs.
* Weather names the presently lived configuration and movement of that field and is not identical with the Fifth.
* The elemental movement is governed by `5 → 4 → 5`: whole → differentiation → representation → reintegration into a transformed whole.
* Difference, opposition, and tension may be generative and need not be eliminated for integration to occur.
* Representation remains subordinate to encounter.
* Human lived experience retains sovereignty over interpretive models.
* MAIA may participate in interpretation but may not substitute a model for the person.
* JARVIS may specialize in explicit representation, verification, provenance, procedure, reproducibility, and bounded execution without acquiring sovereignty over human meaning or purpose.
* Source-established claims, Elemental Alchemy propositions, integrative hypotheses, and empirical findings remain epistemically distinct.
* Coherence, elegance, implementation, or repetition alone cannot convert a proposition or hypothesis into an empirical finding.

**III. REQUIRED NON-SUBSTANTIVE REPAIRS**

No other conceptual revision is authorized under this repair.
