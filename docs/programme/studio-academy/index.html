A way in for each form

Each form is a Studio path you choose to enter. “Just let me write” and “I don’t know yet” stay on every door. Research found that intro tours pushed at people don’t help. What works is learning inside the real document, a little at a time, with help on request.

Every path also gets its own “What do I do next?” answers (the pattern in PR #2344) and a click-through “Show me around” guide on request.

How to ask well

  • Ask at a natural pause, such as after showing MAIA the first page. Never mid-draft.
  • At most once per question wave, as your 1 November design already says, enforced in the code.
  • Stop asking a question once you have enough answers.
  • Always in Soullab’s voice. MAIA never asks for feedback.
  • No click, time or completion tracking. Presses of “What do I do next?” aren’t counted either.
  • Themes may be drafted by a model, but a person checks them before anyone acts.
  • Nothing changes automatically. Every change is a human decision (Invariant 15).
  • Writers see a “What we heard and what changed” page.
The one decision of principle · ruled 19 November: (a)

May Soullab learn from writers’ conversations with MAIA, and how?

(a) The writer shares one exchange at a time your ruling

A “Share this with Soullab” control on an exchange or conversation. Soullab sees only what was handed over. It’s never available in Sanctuary, and it can be withdrawn. Everything not shared stays protected by your 1 November ruling.

(b) A standing research opt-in

Turn on the existing “Research Participation” setting, so Soullab could read all of a consenting writer’s non-Sanctuary conversations. It’s broader and easy to forget once given. It’s also the most criticized pattern in AI products, and it would need a “what you’ve shared” page, a retention limit and regular reminders.
